# Difficult Case Studies

Each case should be independently inspectable and versioned. The purpose is to document a research question and its evidence, not to force a reading.

## Suggested case status

- `proposed`
- `source_verified`
- `under_analysis`
- `candidate_readings`
- `expert_review`
- `tentative_result`
- `unresolved`
- `closed_no_solution`

## Case template

Create a directory such as `cases/OB-0001/` and add a `README.md`:

```markdown
# Case OB-0001 — Short descriptive title

## 1. Research question
What exact graph, phrase, reading, or fragment relation is unresolved?

## 2. Object and source
- Artifact identifier:
- Catalogue number:
- Fragment:
- Excavation/collection:
- Primary source:
- Bibliographic references:
- Access date:

## 3. Rights and asset provenance
- Rights holder:
- License/terms:
- Redistribution allowed:
- Training use allowed:
- Image asset IDs and hashes:

## 4. Direct observations
Describe visible strokes, shape, orientation, damage, lacunae, and segmentation uncertainty without asserting a reading.

## 5. Existing scholarship
List published readings, authors, dates, exact page/plate/catalogue locators, and disagreements.

## 6. Comparable occurrences
For each parallel: artifact, locator, image/source, graph form, proposed reading, and relevance.

## 7. Candidate hypotheses
For each candidate:
- proposed identification/reading;
- supporting visual evidence;
- contextual fit;
- counter-evidence;
- alternative explanations;
- confidence/status and rationale.

## 8. Context
Discuss syntax, divination formulae, parallel texts, archaeological context, and historical implications separately.

## 9. AI and computational experiments
- task:
- dataset/split:
- preprocessing:
- model/provider and exact model ID:
- prompt/code version:
- parameters:
- results:
- failures:
- cost/latency:
- reproducibility instructions:

## 10. Human review
Record reviewer role, date, assessment, disagreements, and unresolved questions.

## 11. Current assessment
State what is supported, what remains uncertain, and what new evidence could change the assessment.

## 12. References
Provide verifiable bibliographic entries and stable links.
```

## Case quality bar

A case is ready for public discussion when its source is verified, its rights are understood, its observations are separated from interpretations, and its alternatives are documented. “Unresolved” is a valid research result.
