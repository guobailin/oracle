## Datasets and computational research

### HUST-OBC
- Paper: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-024-03807-x
- Repository: https://github.com/Pengjie-W/HUST-OBC
- Research relevance: large-scale Oracle Bone Script character image dataset, including identified and unidentified categories.
- Rights note: inspect the repository and dataset license and any underlying image restrictions before reuse.

### OBIMD
- Paper: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-06967-0
- Research relevance: multimodal dataset for contextual interpretation, including image, character, gap, and sentence-level annotations.
- Rights note: the paper's stated license and the dataset's actual reuse terms must be checked independently. Do not assume unrestricted commercial training or redistribution.

### AlphaOracle
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xinn.2026.101462
- Research relevance: AI-assisted decipherment approach inspired by human scholarly workflow, with morphology and contextual evidence.
- Follow-up: verify code availability, data sources, exact evaluation protocol, and license.

### Open-Oracle
- Repository: https://github.com/Yuliang-Liu/Open-Oracle
- Research relevance: curated index of datasets, papers, benchmarks, and code.
- Follow-up: use as a discovery map and verify each linked resource at its original source.

## Resource intake checklist

For every resource added:
- record canonical title, organization, URL, and access date;
- identify the underlying data owner and rights holder;
- record license and whether commercial use, model training, and redistribution are allowed;
- record whether access is public, registered, institutional, or paid;
- distinguish metadata access from image/data download permission;
- document citation and attribution requirements;
- do not mirror content unless explicitly permitted.

## Research gaps to investigate

- Standardized graph-occurrence and uncertainty annotation.
- Artifact-level train/test splits for visual recognition.
- Search across graph forms, parallel inscriptions, and scholarly readings.
- A transparent benchmark for genuinely unresolved and damaged cases.
